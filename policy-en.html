VocalX Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Who we are

VocalX is an offline audio-separation app for Android, developed by Ali-Alinejad (“we”, “the developer”). It is distributed through Cafe Bazaar, Myket and Google Play.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at: alialinejad72@gmail.com.

2. Audio files and processing

VocalX processes audio files locally on your device. Your selected audio files are not uploaded to our servers.

The app uses the file picker to access the audio file that you select. It does not need access to all files on your device.

Audio data decoded for processing is temporary. The app removes temporary processing data after the task is complete.

3. Output files

Separated audio tracks are stored on your device. These files remain private to your device unless you choose to share them.

4. AI model

VocalX downloads its audio-separation model over HTTPS when required. The model is stored locally on your device and is used for offline processing.

When you download the model, the hosting provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser or device user-agent information.

5. Recent results and storage

VocalX keeps up to 10 recent tracks, subject to a storage limit of 700 MB. Older results may be removed automatically when these limits are reached.

VocalX does not include Android cloud backup for its app data.

6. Advertising

VocalX uses the Tapsell advertising SDK to display ads. Tapsell and its advertising partners may process technical and advertising-related data to provide and measure ads.

This data may include your Google Advertising ID, device model, Android version, language, screen information, mobile carrier, IP address, approximate location, app package name, and ad impressions or clicks.

Tapsell may share relevant data with advertising partners and process data on servers located in Iran. Please review Tapsell's privacy policy for more information about its data practices.

7. Permissions

VocalX requests permissions that are needed for its features and for the advertising SDK. These may include network access, network-state information, wake locks, and other permissions required by the SDK.

Audio file access is limited to files that you select through the Android file picker.

8. Analytics and crash reporting

VocalX does not use a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

9. Children's privacy

VocalX is not designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices and rights

You can use VocalX without a network connection once setup is complete, delete results at any time, and reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

For privacy questions or requests about data that the developer may hold, contact us at alialinejad72@gmail.com.

For data processed by Tapsell for advertising, please use the contact details provided in Tapsell's privacy policy.

11. Data security and retention

VocalX stores processing files and results locally on your device. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any audio files that you export or share.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. Please review this page from time to time.
